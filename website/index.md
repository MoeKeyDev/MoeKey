# MoeKey

> Browse. Share. With MoeKey.

MoeKey is an open-source, cross-platform Misskey client built with Flutter. Its interface follows Misskey's familiar style, so you can browse, post, and interact across your devices.

- Official website: https://moekey.moegirl.love/
- Source code: https://github.com/MoeKeyDev/MoeKey
- Website languages: 中文, English, 日本語.

## Features

- **Four timelines:** Home, local, social, and global timelines with pagination and live updates.
- **Posting and interaction:** Attach files, reply, quote renote, create and vote in polls, set content warnings and visibility, renote, react with emoji, and manage your own notes.
- **Profiles and accounts:** Edit your avatar, banner, bio, and custom profile fields. Sign in to multiple accounts and switch between them.
- **Following and notifications:** Browse profiles and follow lists, handle follow requests, and view grouped notifications, mentions, and achievement-earned notifications.
- **Drive and clips:** Upload, organize, and manage files. Create, edit, and favorite clips to collect notes.
- **Search and discovery:** Search notes and users, explore popular content and hashtags, and read server announcements.
- **Media:** Preview and save images, play videos, and reuse video sessions between timelines, note details, and full-screen previews.
